QuizForgeAI · feature constellation

From quiz generator to learning companion.

A divergent brainstorm across learning science, trust, collaboration, game design, accessibility, and implementation constraints.

Core thesis: the product becomes much more valuable when every answer feeds the next learning action — explain the mistake, calibrate confidence, identify the concept, and schedule the right review.

Highest-leverage directions

Learning loop

Confidence-aware practice

Ask how confident the learner is before feedback. Track overconfidence and underconfidence separately from correctness.

Retention

Concept mastery map

Replace one final score with a map of concepts: mastered, shaky, unseen, and worth revisiting.

Trust

Source-grounded quizzes

Generate from notes, pasted text, URLs, or documents, then show citations and source freshness beside claims.

Feedback

Misconception repair

Explain why each distractor is wrong and follow a wrong answer with a tiny lesson plus one targeted retry.

Replayability

Spaced review

Turn weak questions into a daily mix of due reviews, new material, and confidence-calibration challenges.

Quality

Question curation

Detect duplicates, flag ambiguity, approve good generated questions, and keep a reusable personal question bank.

More distinctive bets

Teach-back mode

After answering, ask the learner to explain the idea in their own words and evaluate the explanation.

Scenario mode

Test application through realistic decisions rather than only factual recall.

Question-type expansion

Add ranking, matching, ordering, short answer, and “write the question” modes behind one common model.

Daily question mix

Generate a short daily session from recent mistakes, due reviews, and one surprising new topic.

Teacher and creator workspace

Let people edit AI drafts, set learning goals, share links, and review aggregate performance.

Co-op challenge rooms

Run synchronized rounds for study groups, classrooms, or interview preparation teams.
