- (idea by coach) Explain why each distractor is wrong, not only why the correct answer is right.

- (insight) The strongest product direction is to turn QuizForgeAI from a quiz generator into a learning loop: diagnose knowledge, test it, explain mistakes, schedule weak concepts, and show mastery over time.
- (insight) Trust is a differentiator for AI-generated quizzes: source provenance, freshness dates, confidence signals, distractor explanations, duplicate detection, and user flagging should be designed together.
- (insight) The best first expansion is confidence-aware adaptive practice because it adds learning value without requiring accounts, social features, or a large new content model.
- (direction) Recommended sequence: confidence and concept-level review; then saved history plus spaced repetition; then source-grounded quizzes; then teacher and multiplayer workflows.
